Educational Resources

Malaria prevention & symptoms protocol
  • What is Malaria? A life-threatening disease caused by Plasmodium parasites transmitted through bites of infected female Anopheles mosquitoes.
  • Key Prevention: Use insecticide-treated mosquito nets (ITNs), indoor residual spraying (IRS), and preventive medications when traveling.
  • Seek Care: If you develop high fever, chills, or headache, immediately consult a healthcare professional.
